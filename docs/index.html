FamineWatch Datasets — Famine Explorer

A compilation of documented famines and food-system disruptions, combined with openly licensed famine datasets: the European regional chronology of Ljungqvist, Seim & Collet (2024), Our World in Data's global famine dataset, India's major drought famines (Mishra & Aadhar 2021), Korean, Japanese and Qing-China records. Version 0.2 (beta) · Center for Climate Systems Research, Columbia Climate School.
