A compilation of documented famines and food-system disruptions, plus the regional famine-year chronology for medieval and early modern Europe (1250–1800) of Ljungqvist, Seim & Collet (2024). Version 0.2 (beta) · Center for Climate Systems Research, Columbia Climate School.
